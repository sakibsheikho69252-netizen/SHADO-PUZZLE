How to Play

1
SELECT AN OBJECT
Tap any glowing shape on the board. A dashed ring marks the selected object.
2
MOVE IT
Drag with one finger to slide the object. Watch the shadow move and change size.
3
ROTATE & SCALE
Use the ↺ ↻ buttons to turn in 15° steps, and − + to resize. Pinch and twist with two fingers for the same effect.
4
STUDY THE LIGHT
The light source projects every object onto the dark plane. Closer objects cast larger shadows. Mirrors create a reflected twin shadow.
5
MATCH THE TARGET
Fill the dashed cyan silhouette with your shadow. The match meter rises as you get closer.
6
COMPLETE THE PUZZLE
Hold a match of 90% or more and the level locks in. Fewer moves and no hints earn three stars.
